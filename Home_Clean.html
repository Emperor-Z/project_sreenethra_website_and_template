Our Specialized Departments

We offer comprehensive eye care across nine specialized departments, each equipped with advanced technology and expert specialists.

Cataract Surgery

Advanced cataract surgery using state-of-the-art phaco machines and premium intraocular lenses for optimal vision restoration.

Retina & Vitreous

Minimally invasive vitreoretinal surgery for retinal detachment, diabetic retinopathy, and macular disorders.

Cornea & Refractive Surgery

LASIK, TransPRK, ICL surgery, and corneal transplants using advanced femtosecond laser technology.

Glaucoma

Comprehensive glaucoma management including MIGS procedures, trabeculectomy, and advanced diagnostic tools.

Paediatric Ophthalmology

Specialized care for children including strabismus correction, pediatric cataract surgery, and ROP screening.

Uvea

Advanced treatment of uveitis and inflammatory eye diseases with comprehensive diagnostic and therapeutic approaches.

Neuro-Ophthalmology

Specialized care for optic nerve disorders, visual pathway problems, and neurological eye conditions.

Oculoplasty

Eyelid, lacrimal, and orbital surgery including ptosis repair, blepharoplasty, and artificial eye prostheses.

General Ophthalmology

Comprehensive eye care including routine screening, refractive errors, and coordination with all subspecialties.
